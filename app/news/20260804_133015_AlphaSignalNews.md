OpenAI's unreleased model cracks 10 open math problems for $2,000 in compute
10,791 Likes
OpenAI's next model,
Astra
, just solved
10 open math problems
that nobody had cracked in over a decade. That's the debut.
Astra did not just guess answers. Humans used the model to write up the results, then Astra formalized each proof in Lean, a language for machine-verified theorems. Think of Lean as a strict computer checker that confirms the math is airtight.
The solved problems include:
The first-ever explicit construction of a non-sofic group, resolving a central question in group theory open since 1999.
A disproof of Connes's rigidity conjecture, new bounds for high-dimensional sphere packing, and results resolving several problems posed by Paul Erdos.
The cost? About
$2,000 total
at Sol's API rates. That is $200 per solved problem.
OpenAI published a 249-page manuscript collection, model-written reasoning walkthroughs, and Lean 4 certificates for all 10 results so you can read exactly how it got there.

OpenAI rebuilds ChatGPT Voice so reasoning runs without interrupting speech
7,051 Likes
Old ChatGPT Voice had a clunky problem: it waited for you to stop talking, then thought, then replied. One side at a time. That awkward pause? That was the system guessing when your turn ended.
GPT-Live fixes this.
It uses a
full-duplex
architecture, meaning it listens and speaks at the same time, just like a real conversation. It removes the old turn detector entirely, so the model can listen and speak simultaneously.
Here is what that unlocks:
Hard questions get quietly handed to GPT-5.5 in the background, while the conversation keeps flowing.
Live translation becomes possible because the model does not need a complete sentence before generating output.
You pick reasoning depth: Instant, Medium, or High, each routing to a different GPT-5.5 tier.
Session startup dropped from
six network round trips to one
, making it feel fast from the first word.

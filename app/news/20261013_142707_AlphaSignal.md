Z.ai releases GLM-Legal, a model that matches frontier models on legal tasks
5,055 Likes
Z.ai just dropped
GLM-Legal
, an AI model built specifically for legal work. Think of it as a regular AI that went to law school and actually paid attention.
It runs on GLM-5.3-Flash and was trained on massive legal datasets, then practiced on tens of thousands of real legal task simulations. The result: it can actually complete legal tasks end-to-end, not just answer questions about law.
The headline number is a
19.2% all-pass rate on Harvey LAB
, a strict benchmark covering 24 areas of legal practice. That tripled the base model's 6.7% score. It also costs just
$0.15 per task
, compared to $10.55 for Gemini 4 Argon.
Here's what you can build with it:
Contract review that flags risky clauses and suggests fixes
Evidence analysis across large document sets
Cross-border compliance planning
You can access it via API or try it inside
AutoClaw
, Z.ai's desktop agent app that turns goals into automated multi-step tasks. API access requires applying through their consultation page.

Runs on a
single GPU
(under 16GB VRAM), no server farm needed

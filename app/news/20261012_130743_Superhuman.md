## **How to turn a boring company presentation into an animated explainer with Claude**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/005fab95-98da-4536-b149-02936e05ef82/Group_40__1_.jpg?t=1791756201)
Follow image link: (https://archive.superhuman.ai/2108271552991252810)
Caption: Click here for a quick overview of Claude’s new Motion feature, now in beta. Photo: Anthropic


--------------------
* Open [Claude](https://claude.ai/new) and upload the presentation.

* Click ‘Output’ in the message box and select ‘Motion’, or open ‘Artifacts’ > ‘Motion’.  See how to do this [here](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2108271552991252810).

* Tell Claude who the explainer is for and ask it to keep only the most important ideas.

**Sample Prompt:** _“Turn this presentation into a 60–90 second animated explainer for [audience]. Keep only the most important ideas, simplify jargon, and build a clear narrative from problem to takeaway. Use concise on-screen text, visual metaphors, animated charts, and smooth transitions. Keep the style [professional / playful / premium / minimal] and make every scene easy to understand without the original deck.”_

* Let Claude build the animation using text, charts, shapes, and images from your presentation.

* Review the animation and ask Claude to shorten dense scenes, simplify visuals, or emphasize any numbers and conclusions that matter most.

* Click ‘Export’ and choose ‘MP4’ when the explainer is ready.

## **Mark Zuckerberg and the US government are teaming up to build a “virtual cell” **


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/aebb6138-0992-4bef-88a6-203dc0cb52e9/superhumanteam_A_robot_scientist_holding_up_a_vial_of_liquid__58ed00f8-8835-4599-ad06-fc03a24121e0_2.jpg?t=1791730815)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
🧬** Operation virtual cell**. [Biohub](https://biohub.org/), a nonprofit backed by Mark Zuckerberg, is on a mission to build what it calls a “universal virtual cell.” It has now raised a collective $1.8B to make that happen, joining forces with the US Department of Energy, the National Institutes of Health, DeepMind, and Isomorphic Labs. 

🤔** What’s a virtual cell?** It’s an AI model that understands human biology and simulates how human cells behave. In theory, it could predict how cells respond to a treatment, letting researchers test thousands of hypotheses digitally without needing to set foot in a lab.

### AI startups are pushing deeper into biology, medicine, and science

* **Isomorphic Labs is eyeing a $40B valuation: **The Google DeepMind spinout led by Demis Hassabis is reportedly [raising](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/alphabet-isomorphic-labs-funding-talks-040000989.html?guccounter=1) fresh funding for its AI-powered drug discovery.

* **Midjourney is building a medical scanner:** The lab primarily known for AI image creation is working on a new type of full-body [ultrasound scanner](https://www.midjourney.com/medical/blogpost).

* **Jeff Dean wants to automate the scientific method:** Google’s chief scientist left after 27 years to co-found Discovery Loop, which plans to test experiments en masse using AI.

The list goes on, with startups like [Chai Discovery](https://www.chaidiscovery.com/), [Lila Sciences](https://www.lila.ai/), and [Enveda](https://enveda.com/) all chasing similar goals. While none has successfully brought AI-generated medicine to market yet, that milestone may not be far off.

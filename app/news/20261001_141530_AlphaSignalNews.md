Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 4 Argon with 1M token output for complex workflows
38,544 Likes
Google just dropped
Gemini 4 Argon
, its most powerful model yet. But here's the catch: you can't use it yet.
Right now it's only available to vetted cybersecurity defenders through the
Fairwind Program
, a limited-access channel for governments and trusted partners. Broader access for everyone else is coming soon.
So what makes it special? The biggest deal is the output limit. Previous Gemini models could write up to 64,000 tokens in one response. Argon pushes that to
1 million output tokens
, meaning it can reason through massive, multi-step problems without stopping. Think full codebase migrations, long legal documents, or complex security audits in a single pass.
Here's what it can do:
Autonomously find, validate, and patch critical software vulnerabilities without human help
Handle real-world software engineering across 20 programming languages
Work through legal, financial, and enterprise knowledge tasks end to end
Pricing starts at
$2 per million input tokens
and $10 per million output tokens. Sit tight for general access.

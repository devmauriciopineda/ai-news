This Week in 60 Seconds
Mistral Previews a Trillion-Parameter Model and Promises Open Weights
Mistral Large 4 [ https://substack.com/redirect/d032a277-e2e2-4b92-b81e-2f74d0dbf2cf?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], a model with about one trillion parameters, entered public preview on Tuesday, and Mistral says it will release the weights by the end of the month. Until then, the model is available through Mistral’s preview API, at a launch price [ https://substack.com/redirect/253116a6-d3a9-4686-af01-9cbf0bd2f91c?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] of $0.68 per million input tokens and $2.09 per million output tokens. In a blind test that Mistral ran with Surge AI, human raters scored the model’s code 3.74 out of 5, ahead of the Chinese open-weight models Kimi K3, GLM-5.3, and GLM-5.2 and behind Claude Opus 5 at 4.22. The announcement names no license. Reflection AI also announced an open model on Monday, Beam [ https://substack.com/redirect/0eac713e-bab0-41d3-80f2-28082fc2ec46?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], with weights due later in October. We compare both models with China’s open models in this week’s Deeper Look.
Anthropic Releases Claude Haiku 5.5 with Prices up to 90% Lower
Claude Haiku 5.5 [ https://substack.com/redirect/2d5a7033-0800-4db3-b34b-b9189ca11b3d?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], released on Wednesday, costs $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts of up to 100,000 tokens, a tenth of the $1 and $5 that Anthropic charges for Haiku 4.5. Longer prompts cost $0.50 and $2.50, half the old price. On average, Anthropic says, the saving is about 75%, because about one request in ten is longer than 100,000 tokens and a new tokenizer uses slightly more tokens for the same work. Haiku 5.5 is the first Haiku model with an adjustable effort setting, and it scores above OpenAI’s GPT-6 Luna on the benchmarks Anthropic published. Anthropic also halved the price of cache reads for Claude Sonnet 5.5, from $0.20 to $0.10 per million tokens. Read more about Claude Haiku 5.5 [ https://substack.com/redirect/030fd7b7-35c6-4ab7-8890-afd29030e0a6?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] on our blog.
OpenAI Will Watermark ChatGPT Text in the EU
Text that ChatGPT and Codex generate for users in the European Union will carry an invisible watermark within weeks, OpenAI said [ https://substack.com/redirect/f76b75e6-8a4f-4b04-aa9b-88cf40bb2257?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] on Monday. The EU AI Act [ https://substack.com/redirect/f42b43bb-21f2-4092-a8ec-7556f594f9c1?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] requires providers to make AI-generated text identifiable by machines. API customers anywhere can opt in, and the watermark stays off for ChatGPT users outside the EU. In OpenAI’s tests, at a 1% false-positive rate, the detector found the watermark in about 95% of 400-token passages on a subject such as psychology, and in fewer on mathematics. Replacing a quarter of the words with synonyms cut detection to about 17%. For now, only approved researchers and expert organizations can apply to use the detector.
Finland Orders Google to Stop Work at Two Data Center Sites
Work that alters the land at two planned Google data center sites in northern Finland, in Muhos and Kajaani, must stop by October 23 at the latest. The country’s Licensing and Supervision Agency issued the order [ https://substack.com/redirect/5b6cf9a3-cdaa-4e9d-bd64-09a688a3fc3d?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] to Google’s project company, Tuike Finland, because tree felling and topsoil stripping began before the environmental impact assessments were complete. At Muhos alone, about 300 hectares of forest had been cut down, the agency said in September [ https://substack.com/redirect/1607dc2f-7423-4ca4-a555-e43e52ffc854?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], citing public reports. Both sites belong to a €13 billion investment that Google announced [ https://substack.com/redirect/27f3e098-16a6-47cc-8c5a-b08684aba21e?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] last month and calls its largest in Europe. Google acknowledged [ https://substack.com/redirect/b1882cae-4a38-4d41-a4f8-d3d80b46ec59?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] that it had not met its own standards and plans to plant trees on 130 hectares at Muhos.
Wikimedia Says OpenAI Agents Edited Its Wikis and Strained Its Servers
Wikipedia’s parent, the Wikimedia Foundation, has found edits on its sites that it believes were made by AI agents OpenAI operated, it said [ https://substack.com/redirect/6e42dc27-1706-49e0-9d18-0123416d4a4e?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] on Monday. None of the agents had the approval that Wikimedia requires of automated editors. Most of the edits were on practice pages set aside for testing, but some changed the settings of a citation tool, which Wikimedia thinks the agents meant to use to fetch data from other websites. The agents also flooded Wikimedia’s servers with millions of automated requests, traffic that “may have contributed” to a partial outage of one of its data services in May. Wikimedia found no evidence that its systems or data were compromised.

A Deeper Look at This Week’s News: Can Mistral and Reflection Compete with China’s Open Models?
Between January and July, the largest open-weight model that a Chinese lab released each month had between 754 billion and 2.78 trillion parameters. In five of those seven months, no US lab released a model above 130 billion parameters, according to a Hugging Face report [ https://substack.com/redirect/14775d8c-eeb3-4bdf-926f-dc19f3e0db35?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ].
This week, two labs outside China announced models that approach the Chinese size class. Mistral Large 4 [ https://substack.com/redirect/d032a277-e2e2-4b92-b81e-2f74d0dbf2cf?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], from France, has 1.05 trillion parameters, which would make it the largest open-weight model from outside China. Beam [ https://substack.com/redirect/b9497a57-6c1f-4414-ad4e-bf6d3375ecf3?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], announced by the US startup Reflection AI, has 501 billion.
But size is only one of four things the Chinese lead rests on. On the other three, which are capability, price, and the work developers have already built on Chinese models, the gap is still open. Licenses are the one area where Western labs may have an opening.
Chinese open models start to feel competition on size
Until this week, the open-weight models above one trillion parameters were Chinese. Moonshot’s Kimi K3 has about 2.8 trillion, and Alibaba’s biggest Qwen 3.8 model has 2.4 trillion. The largest open-weight model from a Western lab was Thinking Machines’ Inkling [ https://substack.com/redirect/5bea7171-9cab-4d5c-a1f7-a79714b47931?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], with 975 billion.
Europe’s Mistral Large 4 puts a Western lab above one trillion for the first time, but it doesn’t use its parameters all at once. Like other models of its size, Large 4 is a mixture-of-experts [ https://substack.com/redirect/e80a178b-a557-4101-93f2-6b2d64d9ef37?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] model: its parameters are divided among many specialist sub-networks (the experts), and only a few of them run for each token the model reads or writes, so 52 billion parameters [ https://substack.com/redirect/253116a6-d3a9-4686-af01-9cbf0bd2f91c?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] are active at a time.
Beam, an American model, is about half the size of Large 4. It has 501 billion parameters, with 23 billion active per token, and it handles text only. It is the third-largest open model from a US lab, after Inkling and NVIDIA’s Nemotron 3 Ultra, which has 550 billion.
Neither Mistral Large 4 nor Beam is available to download yet. Both labs say the weights will be released later this month. Until then, Large 4 runs through Mistral’s preview API and Beam through a waitlist.
Mistral narrows the capability gap, but costs four times as much
Artificial Analysis, an independent benchmarking firm, tested the Large 4 preview on the day it came out. The model scored 38 [ https://substack.com/redirect/c9887479-c101-405a-a5c9-6f409f1e44a5?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] on the firm’s Intelligence Index, a single score that combines several tests. That makes it, in the firm’s words, the most intelligent model from outside the US and China.
Several Chinese open-weight models, however, still score higher on the same index, led by Xiaomi’s MiMo-V2.6-Pro at 46.
On price, Large 4 sits between the Chinese models. Artificial Analysis measures price as what each model costs to complete one of its tasks. For Large 4, the figure is $1.13 at list price. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, a Chinese open model that scores 39, costs $0.27, about a quarter as much. Mistral’s two-week launch discount brings Large 4 down to $0.57, which is still about twice the DeepSeek figure.
Beam has no independent results so far. Reflection says the model matches Z.ai’s GLM-5.2 on reasoning tests while using three to four times less compute. Its announcement also says that Kimi K3 remains ahead on capability.
A trillion parameters doesn’t bring developers with it
Large 4 and Beam put Mistral and Reflection closer to the Chinese size class, but building a large model does not by itself make an open lab competitive. China’s own labs show this: the ones that publish only very large models are downloaded far less than the one that also publishes small ones.
According to Hugging Face [ https://substack.com/redirect/14775d8c-eeb3-4bdf-926f-dc19f3e0db35?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], Moonshot (the maker of Kimi) publishes almost nothing below 70 billion parameters, and its models were downloaded only 37 million times in the first seven months of the year. For comparison, Alibaba’s Qwen family, which starts below one billion parameters, was downloaded 2.05 billion times, about 55 times as often. (Downloads are a partial measure, since they don’t capture use through APIs, Hugging Face notes.)
Developers also build on Qwen. Hugging Face counts 151,448 derivatives of it (models that someone has retrained or repackaged from the original). Google comes second with 82,506, and its Gemma family is the one Western rival at this end of the market: versions of Gemma packaged to run on a personal computer are downloaded 20.8 million times a month, against 39.6 million for Qwen.
Where Western labs may have an opening
Licenses are one place where Western labs could gain ground, because this summer the two largest Chinese models came with conditions.
Kimi K3’s license [ https://substack.com/redirect/a3c98e1a-c3cc-4611-ae91-cb69be2d52ca?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ], from July, still lets anyone download the model, retrain it, and build it into a product. But a company that sells access to the model as a service, and whose group earns more than $20 million over 12 months, must first sign a separate agreement with Moonshot. Alibaba’s license [ https://substack.com/redirect/00c0a3cd-579a-44d6-aa0a-69b89783c4ff?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] for its 2.4-trillion-parameter Qwen 3.8 sets the line at $50 million and also covers standalone coding and office assistants.
On the Western side, Reflection says Beam’s weights will come under Apache 2.0, which is one of the most permissive licenses in common use. Mistral hasn’t named a license for Large 4, and VentureBeat reported [ https://substack.com/redirect/f8e0fae9-bfe9-4ea1-9ece-5e257b6e8c2f?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ] that a custom one is expected.
We will watch two things in the coming weeks: how many developers build on Large 4 and Beam, and which license Mistral chooses.
Industry Use Cases
OpenAI Publishes a Collection of Math Proofs Written by an Unreleased AI Model
OpenAI published a set of new mathematical results produced by an internal AI model that isn’t available to the public, so mathematicians can check its work. The release includes versions of many proofs checked in the Lean language, and OpenAI says an average result used compute comparable to about three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking. The remaining proofs have no formal check yet, and the model has no release date. Read more in this article from OpenAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/c0d4b8ea-9f14-44fb-b874-c13fd4f3d30e?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ].
Google Launches Playground, a Platform Where AI Builds Video Games from Text Prompts
Google launched Playground, an experimental browser platform where people describe a game in a chat and AI generates a playable version. Creators can change the rules, characters, and physics by asking, then share the game by link or publish it to a public gallery, with leaderboards and multiplayer in some genres. It is open only to adults in the US, and how much people can create depends on their Google AI subscription. Read more in this article from Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/7b943f64-947a-47ea-ac6f-6e1e1e5723e4?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ].
A Johns Hopkins Astrophysicist Uses Claude AI Agents to Map the Full Ultraviolet Sky
Astrophysicist Brice Ménard, of Johns Hopkins and Anthropic, used Claude to coordinate AI agents that combined data from six space missions into a complete ultraviolet map of the sky. The main survey, GALEX, covered about two-thirds of the sky, so a machine-learning model predicted the rest from visible, infrared, and radio data, landing within about 10% of real measurements in tests. The agents missed a visible flaw through two review rounds, until Ménard spotted it. Read more in this article from Anthropic [ https://substack.com/redirect/361d8029-ad6c-4a4c-b9c1-f179967f3659?j=eyJ1IjoiMndmMTJ4In0.oZAfFOSrevILB5Hnb24UxZa264EhpHj0bs0Bu33iYKY ].

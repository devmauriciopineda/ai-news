## **How to audit spreadsheet formulas for errors with Claude for Google Workspace**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/4039383f-3675-49a1-9d3c-0bdfa3343ba4/image_-_2026-10-08T023410.827.jpg?t=1791408867)
Caption: Photo: Sefa Aydin


--------------------
* Open your [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/) and launch [Claude for Google Workspace](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors) from the sidebar.

* Tell Claude what the spreadsheet is for and which tabs or totals matter most.

* Ask it to check for broken formulas, hard-coded values, missing formulas, inconsistent ranges, and totals that don’t reconcile.

**Sample Prompt: **_“Audit this spreadsheet for formula errors and inconsistencies. Flag broken references, hard-coded values, missing formulas, inconsistent logic, copied formulas that look wrong, and totals that don’t reconcile. List each issue by sheet and cell, explain why it may be wrong, and suggest the safest fix without changing anything yet.”_

* Review Claude’s findings. Ask it to separate likely errors from cells that simply need human review.

* Approve the confirmed fixes and ask Claude to update only those cells.

* Run the audit again to confirm the corrected formulas and key totals now reconcile.

## **“Hybrid intelligence” took center stage at Microsoft’s first laptop-focused event in two years**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/42403c00-ccfd-4e9f-b706-98e51d31a8ba/superhumanteam_Satya_Nadella_on_stage_showcasing_a_new_laptop_ee0391a8-ed10-44c7-8680-15b537066454_1.jpg?t=1791416177)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**What’s hybrid intelligence?** It describes AI that runs either on your device or in the cloud, depending on the task. Smaller tasks stay local on your device, while bigger tasks are sent to stronger cloud-based computers. 

This concept was the centerpiece of Microsoft's Surface Laptop Ultra event, where CEO Satya Nadella laid out a vision to turn every Windows computer into a place where agents work on your behalf. Here's what to know:

* **Microsoft’s new laptop is built for local AI and 3D graphics:** The [Surface Laptop Ultra](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/surface/devices/surface-laptop-ultra), powered by a Nvidia RTX Spark Superchip, can run agents, generate assets, and automate repetitive tasks, all on the device.

* **Windows Search and Copilot can take action for you:** Type basic commands like “dim my screen” and Windows Search will get it done. Or, use Copilot as your on-device assistant to take action across the computer while tapping into local context.

* **New safeguards keep agents away from sensitive files:** [Microsoft Execution Containers](https://github.com/microsoft/mxc) let you wall off agents in a designated area, limiting which files and networks they can access.

💻️ **Entering the fray.** The new Surface Laptop Ultra will compete against Apple’s MacBook Pro, Googlebook, and several AI-first personal computer startups like Ghost’s [Core](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2107146556176798081) and [Oki Home](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2107504364168597863).

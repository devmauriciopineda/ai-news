* Open [Higgsfield AI Influencer](https://higgsfield.ai/ai-influencer-studio) > ‘Create New’ to start building your character.

* Customize the influencer’s age, appearance, face, body, hair, style, and accessories, or upload a reference photo if you want to preserve a specific look.

**Sample prompt: **_“Recreate this reference video using my AI influencer. ## **OpenAI caused a stir when it solved one decades-old math problem. Now it’s back with 700+ new results.**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/52e1577c-6734-44b7-bc40-bd46449e72b3/superhumanteam_A_robot_writing_thousands_of_math_answers_on_a_74407888-3529-4ca5-ad8c-c32941b05124_0.jpg?t=1791334822)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
One month ago, an internal OpenAI model helped [find an alleged solution](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2097374640582668336) to one of the most complex problems in math: the Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem. The lab apparently hasn’t taken its foot off the gas since then:

* OpenAI just released [722 manuscripts](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/CONTENTS.md) offering progress on a variety of unsolved problems. 

* The work spans pure mathematics, theoretical computer science, mathematical physics, and other domains.

* The manuscripts are at different stages of verification. Some contain principal results, while others offer companion arguments, consequences, or alternative proofs.

OpenAI says the “vast majority” of these results were generated by an internal model, which spent an average of three hours of compute to reach each result. The findings were released late last night and have yet to be verified by outside mathematicians.

### It’s sure to expand the industry’s growing rift with the math community

As of early September, twenty-eight mathematicians had signed an [open letter](https://mathandai.org/) arguing AI labs are threatening their intellectual work. OpenAI, in particular, has been [accused](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2026-09-10-676a) of using unpublished research without consent or credit to reach its results. 

Even elite mathematicians are now grappling with the same question that artists, engineers, and countless other professions have already faced: what happens to human work when AI can produce the results faster?

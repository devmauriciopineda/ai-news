## **How to turn any topic into an animated explainer video with Grok Bot**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/831d98ba-6157-4b2e-97d6-02e8e252902b/Group_42__3_.jpg?t=1792011419)
Follow image link: (https://archive.superhuman.ai/2110013733527318964)
Caption: Click here to check out an animated explainer video that a user created using Grok Bot. Photo: 


--------------------
* Open [Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot), choose New > Create new Bot. Name it something like ‘Explainer Video Bot’. 

* Attach any source material you want it to use, such as PDFs, links, images, PowerPoints, or reference videos.

**Sample Prompt:**_** **__“Create a 60–90 second animated explainer about [topic] for [audience]. Research the topic carefully, identify the 4–6 ideas someone must understand, and turn them into a clear beginning-to-end story. Write concise narration, then create matching visuals and animations for each scene using simple diagrams, labels, charts, maps, or illustrative sequences where useful. Keep the tone [clear / playful / professional], and make the final video understandable without any prior knowledge.”_

* Let Grok Bot generate the visuals and assemble the narration using its video tools.

* Review the first version and ask the Bot to shorten dense sections, clarify confusing visuals, or add citations for factual claims before generating the final cut.

* Save the finished instructions, sources, and workflow inside the Bot’s setup so you can reopen the same Bot later and run it on a new topic.

## **AI is redefining education quickly. Some are starting to go against the traditional path.**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/dccd0b59-cae8-460a-a5d9-9f8ee6f79d53/superhumanteam_Students_stuck_in_a_classroom_looking_out_a_wi_48bdbc39-2971-4e58-81fc-94302c3f9586_3.jpg?t=1791917914)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
Today’s parents are facing a dilemma (or several, really). Should elementary-age kids still spend all day in a classroom when AI has unlocked cheap, 24/7 personal tutoring? What about college? Are four-year degrees still worth the cost when they could be obsolete by graduation?

There’s no right answer yet. Even if there were, overhauling the education system would take years. But some parents are starting to experiment.

### **Swapping chalkboards for ChatGPT**

OpenAI Advisor Fidji Simo [sends](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2101738984636559708) her daughter to Alpha School, which uses 1:1 AI tutoring to teach kids core subjects in just two hours a day. Per the school’s data, this strategy is [highly effective](https://alpha.school/blog/alphas-mid-year-report-card-is-in-heres-what-the-data-actually-says/#:~:text=The%20Alpha%20District%20Winter,perfect%20SAT%20scores.) and puts it in the top 0.1% globally. The downside is the cost: anywhere from $10K to $75K per year. Others have found success even without expensive private programs:

1. Former Verizon VP Wes Pomeroy built his sons a custom AI setup that turns class materials into study guides that work best for them (here’s [how to build your own](https://wespomeroy.substack.com/p/arming-my-sons-with-ai-the-build)).

2. Venture capitalist Deedy Das says he met a 14-year-old [speedrunning](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2109238984581001353) their career with self-taught AI tutoring.

3. Cartesia engineer Noah Tye [built a system](https://archive.superhuman.ai/2108675665373589841) where GPT-6 Astra monitors his handwritten notes and offers advice, pairing the benefits of writing by hand with AI.

While most families still rely on the traditional classroom — which offers plenty of benefits a chatbot can’t replicate — expect AI-centric education to continue growing in popularity.

39,062 Likes
Anthropic just dropped
Claude Haiku 5.5
, and the headline is the price. It costs
90% less per token
than Haiku 4.5 for most requests, landing at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens.
There is one catch worth knowing before you migrate: that cheap rate only applies to prompts under 100,000 tokens. Go over that, and the price jumps 5x. The good news? About 90% of real requests stay under that limit anyway.
Here is what you can actually do with it now:
Run high-volume tasks like summaries and classification at near-zero cost
Use it as a cheap subagent alongside Sonnet or Opus for coding pipelines
Power live customer support and browser automation, it is fast enough
Tune the
effort setting
(low to max) to trade speed for smarts per task
It also ships with a
1 million token context window
and 128K max output. Available now on AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure via model ID
claude-haiku-5-5

25,270 Likes
ChatGPT just stopped being a text box.
GPT-6 with Intelligent UI
is rolling out now, and it changes what an "answer" actually looks like.
Instead of walls of text, ChatGPT now picks the best format for your question automatically. Ask something complex, and it might hand you a clickable diagram. Ask for help budgeting, and it builds you a working calculator right inside the chat.
Here's what you can actually do with it:
Get
interactive charts and diagrams
that respond as you explore them
Use tappable buttons and forms built on the spot for your specific task
Play games or run calculators generated live in your conversation
Get partial answers faster while the model is still thinking
Two model versions power this:
GPT-6 Sol
for paid tiers,
GPT-6 Luna
for Free and Go. Both handle everyday conversation. This only affects the Chat tab, not Work or Codex.
The big unlock here is that ChatGPT now builds mini-apps around your question on demand. No coding needed.

Google Labs launches Playground, a no-code AI game creation tool
21,415 Likes
Someone just dropped a platform that lets you
build and play your own games
with zero coding required. It's called
Playground
, and the pitch is simple: if you can imagine a game, you can make it.
No engines. No syntax errors. No tutorials to grind through.
Here's what makes it interesting for you:
You describe what you want, and the platform builds it, meaning AI is doing the heavy lifting behind the scenes
It opens up game creation to anyone who has ideas but not the time to learn Unity or Unreal
It's a live experiment in how far
natural language interfaces
(just typing what you want in plain English) can replace traditional tools

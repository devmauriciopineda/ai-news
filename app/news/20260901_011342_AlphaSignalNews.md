Anthropic permanently raises Claude Code weekly limits by 25% starting September 14
21,788 Likes
Heads up: Anthropic just changed the usage limits for
Claude Code
, and the math is a little confusing at first glance.
Here is what is actually happening. There was a temporary 50% boost to weekly limits running right now. That boost ends soon. After that, limits settle at a
permanent 25% increase
over the original baseline.
So compared to where limits are today, you will feel a
17% reduction
. But compared to where limits were before the temporary boost, you are still getting more. Net positive, just not as generous as the temporary spike.
This affects all paid plans:
Pro, Max, Team, and seat-based Enterprise
The good news is Anthropic says they are working on changes that give you better visibility into your usage and more control over it, so you are not flying blind when limits get tight.
For now, plan your Claude Code sessions around the new permanent ceiling.

Grok Bot can now shop online and pay using single-use cards
10,983 Likes
Your AI just got a credit card.
Grok Bot can now shop online for you
, and it is actually pretty clever about how it works.
Here is the setup: connect the
Stripe Link plugin
inside Grok Bot's Plugins section, then tell your Bot what to buy. It searches products, compares options, builds the cart, and heads to checkout on its own.
Now the part everyone asks about: is it safe?
Grok Bot never sees your real card number
Stripe Link creates a
single-use virtual card
for each purchase, then throws it away
You approve every spend request before any money moves, seeing the merchant, amount, and full breakdown
You can set rules: allowed sites, budget limits, what it must never buy
Someone already used it to order a Tesla Model Y. So yes, the ceiling is high.
Currently US only. Mobile approval coming soon.

Claude Code desktop app now lets you resume CLI sessions mid-conversation
7,297 Likes
Ever start a coding session in the terminal, then wish you could move it to a proper app with a nicer interface? That gap just closed.
Claude Code now lets you
resume any terminal session inside the desktop app
. Just type
/resume
in the app, pick the session you want, and it loads up with the full conversation history and context, exactly where you left off.
Why does this matter? Context is everything when coding with AI. Losing it means re-explaining your whole project from scratch. Now you never have to.
Start a session in the terminal, finish it in the app
Full conversation history carries over, nothing gets lost
One command:
/resume
This opens up a smoother workflow where you can jump between environments freely. Terminal for speed, desktop app when you want more comfort. Same brain, different window.

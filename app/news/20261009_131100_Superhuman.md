## **How to find the 20% of actions driving 80% of your progress using Claude**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/93fe306a-2e23-4516-b176-2e2c87988e07/image_-_2026-10-09T021836.472.jpg?t=1791494326)
Follow image link: (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GFCWM3CY?th=1&psc=1&geniuslink=true)
Caption: Click here to check out Ben Meer’s How to be Good at Life. Photo: Penguin Random House


--------------------
* Open [Claude > Projects](https://claude.ai/cowork/projects) for one goal where you want to improve.

* Go to the Connectors tab in Settings and connect your calendar.

* Add your goal, current routine, time available, constraints, and recent progress to the Project instructions.

**Sample Prompt:** _“Audit my calendar. Using the 80/20 rule, identify the three to five actions most likely to create outsized progress toward [goal]. Rank them by expected ROI based on impact, effort, and speed to results. Explain why each action matters, what I should deprioritize, and how to apply each one immediately within [time/budget/other constraints]. Turn the top actions into a simple weekly plan with measurable targets.”_

* At the end of each week, return to the same Project and add what you completed, what worked, and where you got stuck.

* Ask Claude to re-rank your highest-leverage actions for the next week based on your latest results.

👉️ **This idea** is adapted from Ben Meer’s _[How to Be Good at Life](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GFCWM3CY?th=1&psc=1&geniuslink=true)_, a practical guide to help you build simple systems for improving work, health, money, relationships, and more.
